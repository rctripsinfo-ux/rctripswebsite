Agar tumhara Mussoorie jaane ka plan hai na, thoda sa ruk jao, usse thodi si aage jagah ek Kanatal hai, wahan chalte hain, view bhi itna tagda aayega ki Mussoorie bhool jaoge. Humne launch kiya ek Delhi se Kanatal ka weekend trip, sirf 6,800 mein. Friday raat 10 baje Delhi se niklenge aur raaste mein bahut saari games, masti aur full enjoy karne wale hain. Subah Kanatal pahunchkar naashta, aur uske baad seedha Surkanda Devi Temple jaayenge, jahan se poora Himalaya ka view dikhta hai, uske baad bahut saara adventure karenge. Zipline, sky roller, sky cycling aur bahut kuch, jo bhi saari cheezein package mein include rahengi. Uske baad Tehri Lake par banana ride kar sakte hain, aur uske baad Delhi wapsi. Aur poore trip pe ek trip captain aap ke saath rahega, jo aapko ek doosre se connect rakhega, aur koi dikkat bhi nahi hone dega.
