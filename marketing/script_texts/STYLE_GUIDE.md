# RC Trips – Reel Script Style (approved)

## Rules
- Speaker: male (ladka). Use "batata hoon", "kar dunga".
- Write in Roman (Hinglish), the way a normal person talks to friends.
- Simple words. No hard Hindi (e.g. no "ajnabi"); use English words instead.
- No filler or story lines ("sach bataun?" etc.). Go straight into the itinerary.
- Hook (line 1): place + price + "Delhi se weekend". The video's topic must be clear immediately.
- Casual Book of Trips touches: "Toh guys…", "poora plan suno/batata hoon", "games-shames",
  "bhai, yahin rehna hai", "kasam se".
- Keep "poori raat dher saari gappe maarenge".
- CTA: tag a friend + comment "<PLACE>" + "poori details DM kar dunga".
- Length: about 40–50 sec of speech.

## Example (Variant 2 – Kanatal)
Guys, agar is weekend kuch plan nahi hai, toh Kanatal chalo. Sirf ₹6,800 mein poora weekend set hai.
Plan simple hai, suno.
Friday raat 10 baje Delhi se nikalna hai. Raaste mein games, gaane, aur itni masti ki neend hi nahi aayegi.
Subah Kanatal pahunchoge na, toh bahar ka view dekhke bologe "bhai, kahan aa gaye". Naashta karke seedha Surkanda Devi Temple, jahan se poore Himalaya ke darshan hote hain.
Phir aata hai asli maza. Zipline, sky roller, sky cycling aur Horizon Bridge, aur haan, ye sab package mein hi included hai, alag se ek rupaya nahi.
Shaam ko Dhanaulti ka jungle, raat ko bonfire, music, aur poori raat dher saari gappe.
Agle din Tehri Lake pe banana ride, aur Monday office se pehle aap wapas Delhi mein.
Toh dost ko tag karo jiske saath jaana hai, aur comment mein "KANATAL" likho. Poori details DM kar dunga.

## Example (Variant 1 – Kanatal)
Toh guys, ek aisi trip nikaali hai jismein Friday raat niklenge aur Monday office se pehle wapas bhi aa jaayenge. Jagah hai Kanatal, aur price sirf ₹6,800.
Aapko poora plan batata hoon, kaisa rahega. … (same itinerary) …
Toh bolo, chal rahe ho? Comment mein "KANATAL" likho, poori details DM kar dunga.

## RULE: Every script needs a NEW hook (never repeat)
Hooks already used (don't reuse):
- "Toh guys, ek aisi trip nikaali hai jismein…" (Kanatal V1)
- "Guys, is weekend kuch plan nahi hai? Toh chalo…" (Kanatal V2, Rishikesh draft 1)
- "Ganga kinare shaam ki aarti, aur saath mein 20 naye dost…" (Rishikesh V2)
- "Zipline, sky cycling, bonfire aur Himalaya ka view, sab ek weekend mein…" (Kanatal short)
Hook ideas bank: question/pain point ("Office ki thakaan…"), FOMO ("Ye cheez life mein ek baar…"),
budget shock ("₹5,500 mein itna kuch?"), POV ("POV: Friday raat…"), list ("3 cheezein jo…"), challenge ("Bet lagao…").
