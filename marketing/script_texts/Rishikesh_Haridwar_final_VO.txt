Humne launch kiya hai Delhi se Haridwar Rishikesh ek stranger trip, woh bhi sirf 5,500 mein. Saath mein sirf 16-36 saal ke log rahenge aur ek trip captain jo aapko har cheez guide karta chalega aur full masti karata chalega, aur poora trip weekend par rehne wala hai, toh koi bhi office ka jhanjhat nahi rahega. Toh plan suno: Raat ko 11 baje Delhi se niklenge aur subah seedha Haridwar ke ghat pe, jisko Ganga mein dubki lagaani hai woh laga lo, phir jaayenge Mansa Devi ke darshan ke liye. Wahan se seedha hum jaane wale hain Rishikesh hotel mein check in, thoda aaram, uske baad seedha shaam ko Ganga Aarti. Bhai woh diye aur woh mantra, matlab rongte khade ho jaate hain logon ke. Raat ko dinner aur phir bas dher saari gappe maarenge. Agle din subah Ganga kinare yoga aur meditation, phir aaram se naashta karenge, uske baad Ram Jhule pe ghoomne bhi jaayenge, cafe mein baithenge. Rafting ka mann ho toh woh bhi kar sakte hain. Aur haan, travel, hotel, khaana aur yoga sab package mein hi hai, aur poore trip pe ek trip captain aapke saath rahega, jo aapko ek doosre se connect rakhega, aur koi dikkat bhi nahi hone dega.
