**Not available (removed from the plan):** Trip Captain face video, written reviews, team/office videos, trade-fair numbers.

**How we build trust without those:**
1. **Real faces in every post:** our travellers' clips, never stock as the main shot.
2. **Full transparency:** itinerary, what's included, what's extra, all from the PDFs.
3. **Location proof:** location tag + "Real trip, not stock" line.
4. **Consistency:** a post every day with the same look.

**Style:** real clip, one short white serif line with one yellow word, trending song, small logo in the corner. Caption ends with a CTA + bracket keywords (strangers trip, Delhi weekend trip, group trip, trip captain…).

## Pillars
- **Proof:** real trip moments, 40%.
- **Transparency:** itinerary, price, inclusions, 20%.
- **Community / relatable:** 15%.
- **Value:** save-worthy, 10%.
- **Offer:** 15%.
