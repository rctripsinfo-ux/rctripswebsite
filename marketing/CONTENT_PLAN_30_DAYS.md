# RC Trips: 30-day Instagram plan (13 Nov – 12 Dec 2026)
**Goal:** page credibility, i.e. real people, real trips, proof, faces and reviews, plus leads for the **27 Nov batch** (Kanatal 27–29 Nov, Rishikesh 27–29 Nov, Manali 27 Nov–1 Dec).
**Rhythm:** 1 feed post every day (reel or carousel) + 3–5 stories every day. WhatsApp number on everything: **+91 99109 85862**.

## 5 content pillars (why each one builds credibility)
| Pillar | Share | What it proves |
|---|---|---|
| **Proof**: real trip clips, raw, no posters | 35% | "These trips really happen, with real people" |
| **Trust**: Trip Captain face, safety, reviews, team, 10+ yrs of tours (trade-fair delegations 600+ people) | 20% | "This company is genuine, not a fly-by-night" |
| **Community**: Intro Rounds, polls, "tag your trip buddy", collab reels | 15% | "I'll make friends here" |
| **Value**: save-worthy info (packing list, budget, Delhi weekend list) | 15% | "They know travel" (saves + shares) |
| **Offer**: beat ads, dates, "Comment KANATAL" | 15% | Leads |

**Style rules:**
- Real clip, one short line in white serif, one word in yellow, trending song, small logo in the corner.
- No festival posters.
- Every caption ends with a CTA ("Comment X", "Tag", "Save").
- Add bracket keywords at the end of the caption: (strangers trip, Delhi weekend trip, trip captain, group trip …).

**Footage codes:** IMG_xxxx = Drive folder 2 (see `research/DRIVE2_FOOTAGE_CATALOG.md`); kanA/kanB = old Kanatal clips; "Beat ad" = `deliverables/*_Beat_Ad_v2`.
