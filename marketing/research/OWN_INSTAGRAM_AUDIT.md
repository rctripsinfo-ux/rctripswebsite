## Problem for community trips
1. **Audience mismatch:** the bio, the grid and the followers are all business delegates and luxury holidays. A 22-year-old who sees the Kanatal ad and opens the profile finds Diwali posters and China expo group photos.
2. **No real people:** there are almost no trip videos, faces, bus masti or reviews. Competitors' top reels are 60–80% real UGC.
3. **No CTA engine:** no posts say "Comment KANATAL" or similar, so there are no DMs from posts.
4. **44% of posts are festival greetings:** these get the lowest engagement and say nothing about any trip.
5. **Old number:** the old number 72900 12474 is in captions and footers, but new ads use 99109 85862.

## Recommendation
**A. (Recommended) A separate community account,** for example @rctrips.community or @rctrips_strangers. Keep @red_carpet_tours for B2B, trade fairs and luxury. The audience, tone and look are completely different, and a new account starts with a clean grid that matches the ads.

**B. If you keep one account:**
- **Bio:** "Delhi se weekend trips with strangers 🏔️ / Kanatal • Rishikesh • Manali • Vrindavan / Trade fairs & luxury: DM / 👇 WhatsApp 99109 85862".
- **Pin:** the 3 community posts.
- **Highlights:** Strangers Trips, Reviews, Trip Captain, Trade Fairs (move B2B here).
- **Content mix:** 60% community reels (UGC, bus masti, Intro Rounds, comment-keyword), 20% package/offer, 10% trade fair, 10% festival. Make festival posts as trip clips plus a wish, not posters.
- **Visual:** drop the footer strip and the poster look for community posts. Use real clips with one white serif line and one yellow word, and the logo small in a corner.
