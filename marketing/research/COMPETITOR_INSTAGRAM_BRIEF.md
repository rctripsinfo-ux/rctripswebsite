## The 8 content types they all repeat

1. **Collab / traveller reels (biggest reach):** customers, creators and celebrities post their trip vlog as a collab with the brand. 9–16 of each account's top 20 reels are posted by someone else. Captions like "first stranger trip", "strangers to family", "15 strangers turned friends".
2. **"Comment KEYWORD" lead reels and carousels:** "Comment 'Trip' → itinerary in DM", "DM RAJASTHAN PACKAGE". These get 100–1,700 comments, which means leads plus a reach boost.
3. **Relatable memes and skits (office/corporate + travel):** Gen-Z office, manager memes, "post-trip dues", girls vs boys, long-weekend plans. 7–30s, trending Bollywood audio. Highest views for the brand's own page (Desh Videsh 1.4M, Capture 155K).
4. **Save-worthy info:** "Save this before you book", "9 snow places without trek", "best time to visit", real price/rate lists, visa/budget carousels (20 slides), itinerary reels. These drive saves and shares.
5. **Group-fun moments from trips:** dancing, games (Mafia/Traitors), bubble-gum challenge, birthday on the trip, fit check, bus masti. 8–20s, no talking.
6. **Offer / sale / date reels:** Christmas/New Year sale, "Next Trip: This Friday", "3 slots left", dates and price in the reel.
7. **Team / founder / behind the scenes:** Diwali office party, 10-year anniversary, founder irony ("everyone travelling while we work"), office comedy series. Builds trust.
8. **Topical / festival:** Diwali, Dhanteras, Bhai Dooj, long weekend, New Year countdown ("11 days left to decide NYE").

## Format notes
- Reels: median 17–29s (WanderOn's creator vlogs are 50s+). The hook is a 1–4 word caption on screen, plus a trending Hindi song.
- Captions: Hinglish, short, one CTA ("Comment X", "Tag your friend", "Btao chal rhe ho?").
- SEO trick: a keyword list in brackets at the end of the caption: "(strangers trip, trip captain, weekend getaway, Delhi departures …)".
- Carousels: trip photo dumps ("Currently 📍Bali"), itinerary/price slides, quizzes, mythology/facts, giveaways.
- Posting rate: about 30 posts in 10–16 days, so 2–3 posts a day (Book of Trips, JustWravel, Capture, Byko, Desh Videsh).

### Common patterns
- **Most top reels are NOT edited ads.** They are raw phone footage (UGC) or a person talking to camera. Heavy graphics (like our price/icon overlay) are used only in paid ads, not in the reels that go viral organically.
- **3 kinds of reel:** (1) trip clip + trending song + one line of text (Book of Trips, Byko, Boketto); (2) talking head or skit with captions (Capture, Tripgix, WanderOn, Desh Videsh); (3) cinematic montage (JustWravel).
- **Text:** one short line only. Most used look is the thin white **serif** (Instagram's own serif font) with one keyword in yellow or pink. Second most used is the bold sans in a **yellow box** (CapCut style) for memes. Third is a white label box for series titles.
- **Colour:** natural phone colours, no heavy grading. Text is white plus one accent: yellow (most), then pink, green (Capture's brand) or red.
- **Series formats repeat:** Intro Rounds (Book of Trips), Rating X (Capture, Tripgix), Kaam Kal Se episodes (Desh Videsh), Traitors (Boketto), celebrity series (WanderOn).
